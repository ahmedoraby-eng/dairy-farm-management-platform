# Roadmap

Each phase is useful on its own and builds on the previous one.

| Phase | Module | Outcome |
|---|---|---|
| 0 | Foundations | Tenancy, versioned configuration, audit trail, core feeding calculation *(in this repo)* |
| 1 | Feed Management MVP | Configure sites, feeding groups, pens and rations; generate and approve daily plans; printable mixer sheets |
| 2 | Inventory & Stock | Raw-material balances, consumption from approved plans, days of cover, low-stock alerts |
| 3 | Herd integration | Import head counts and group movements from the farm's herd management system through an adapter |
| 4 | Reporting | Cross-site and cross-farm dashboards: consumption, cost per head, plan vs actual, leftovers |
| 5 | Tenant self-service | Tenant admin portal, user and role management, onboarding templates |
| 6 | Optimisation | Analytics and recommendations where data volume and quality justify them |

## Out of scope (for now)

- Replacing specialist herd/veterinary or milk-recording systems. The platform integrates with them first.
- Billing and subscription management. To be decided when the commercial model is set.
